# Design guide — An Autobiography Told Through Love

## Purpose
An interactive personal autobiography told through relationships, memory, grief, friendship, family, and other forms of love.

## Intended feeling
Intimate, reflective, layered, and quietly discoverable.

## Primary interaction
Visitors open doors and reveal memories at their own pace.

## Non-negotiables
- Preserve the interactive doors and memory reveals.
- Preserve emotional nuance and narrative clarity.
- Do not imply every important person is a romantic partner.
- Distinguish romantic heartbreak, friendship, family, grief, memory, and other forms of love when context requires it.
- Prefer subtle clarification over explaining the personal history too heavily.

## Mobile and accessibility
Doors and reveal controls must remain comfortably tappable. Reading order, focus behavior, contrast, and scrolling should support unhurried reading on small screens. Images need useful alternatives when they carry meaning.

## Privacy
This is personal work, but it does not need visitor tracking or data collection by default.

## Current design question
How can the work preserve mystery while giving visitors enough context to understand the different forms of love?

## Non-goals
- Forcing external utility or a conventional portfolio narrative
- Making every relationship romantic
- Standardizing the experience to match unrelated projects
